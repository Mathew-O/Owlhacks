01
Three outcomes

Every ticket ends one of three ways.

The model classifies. A file a human wrote decides what the model is allowed to do with that. Those are two different jobs and they live in two different files.

no clickL1 auto reply
13 / 30

Password, MFA, VPN, printer, Outlook, how-to. Confidence over 0.80. The reply goes out tagged auto.

  • Requester gets the runbook steps, in desk voice
  • Ticket tagged auto, so the rate is measurable
  • Kill switch: AUTO_REPLY=off
no clickL2 / L3 escalate
16 / 30

Access, software, hardware, servers, security, firewall changes, outages. Straight to the right queue.

  • Requester gets one line: what is wrong, who has it
  • Specialist gets summary, suggested fix, runbook refs
  • The model can raise a level, never lower one
one clickneeds a person
1 / 30

Under the confidence gate, a category not on the auto list, or the model wants something from the requester.

  • The draft waits in the dashboard
  • Send, redraft with a note, escalate, skip
  • Every click is one Command(resume=...)
Those counts are a real run of the 30 sample tickets with ollama:gemma3:4b on the Acer. 30 out of 30 levels matched what a human assigned.

06
Demo script

Two minutes, in this order.

0:00Dashboard open, 30 tickets, nothing touched. "This is a Monday morning queue at a two office company."
0:15Press Run agent. Levels appear, the queue sorts itself, counts land at 13 auto, 16 escalated, 1 waiting.
0:45Open an L1 auto reply. Read it out loud. Point at the why line: confident, safe category, sent.
1:05Open the L3. "It did not try to fix an outage. It wrote the incident note and handed over."
1:25Open the one that needs a person. Type a note, Redraft, then Send. That is the human in the loop, in one click.
1:45Ask a judge for a ticket. + New ticket, type it, watch it land. Point at the model chips: Ollama on this laptop, Claude in the cloud, same graph.

Stepping stone

Same shape, three jobs.

The hackathon build is the middle rung. The work version on either side uses the same pattern, the same adapters and the same pause.

v1 · work · firewall changes

Reads the ticket, writes the change, keeps the timer. People review, approve and push.

Five pauses. The agent has zero firewall access.

v2 · work · runbooks

Reads documentation and meeting notes, writes runbooks, keeps them current.

The runbooks this triage agent reads come from there.

v3 · this weekend · ticket triage

Reads the ticket, picks the level, answers L1, escalates L2 and L3.

One pause. The adapter makes it a Freshservice agent on Monday.
