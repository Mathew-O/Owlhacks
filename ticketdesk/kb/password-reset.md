# Password reset and locked accounts

Category: password
Owner: Service Desk (L1). Time: 5 minutes.

Symptoms: "account locked", "password expired", "cannot log in", too many wrong attempts.

1. Confirm identity: call back on the number in the directory, or verify the manager name and employee ID.
2. Self service first: send the requester to the password portal, https://password.northline.com, "Forgot password", MFA code, new password.
3. Locked account: unlock in the admin console (Users > search > Unlock). The lock also clears by itself after 30 minutes.
4. Expired password: unlock, then reset to a temporary password, tick "must change at next sign in", read it over the phone, never by email.
5. Ask the requester to sign out and back in on the laptop while on the office network or VPN so the new password is cached.
6. Note the ticket with what you did and close it.

Escalate if: the requester did not ask for the reset (possible breach), the account is a service or admin account, or the lock repeats within a day (security, L2).
