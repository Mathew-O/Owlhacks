# Access requests: shared drives, apps, new hires, leavers

Category: access_request
Owner: Service Desk logs it, an approver and L2 fulfil it. Time: 1 to 3 business days.

1. Every access request needs an approval on the ticket from the data owner or the manager. "My manager approved it in our 1:1" is not an approval; ask the manager to reply on the ticket.
2. Shared drive: once approved, add the user to the matching AD group (drive name = group name, for example GRP-Finance-RW). Access appears after the next sign in.
3. App role (Freshservice, Adobe, Visio): once approved, L2 assigns the licence or role. Note the cost centre on the ticket.
4. New hire: HR ticket from the manager at least 5 business days before start. Create the account from the template of a person in the same role, order the laptop from stock, add to the office and team groups.
5. Leaver: disable the account on the last day, forward mail to the manager for 30 days, collect the laptop.
6. Tell the requester the expected date and close when done.

Escalate if: the request is for admin rights, finance systems, or production servers (L2 with a second approver).
