# How do I ... Microsoft 365 and Teams

Category: how_to
Owner: Service Desk (L1). Time: 5 minutes. These are answers, not fixes.

1. Share my calendar with details: Outlook > Calendar > right click the calendar > Sharing Permissions > add the person > choose "Can view all details".
2. Record a Teams meeting: in the meeting, More (three dots) > Record and transcribe > Start recording. The recording lands in the organiser's OneDrive under Recordings, and in the meeting chat.
3. Set an out of office: Outlook > File > Automatic Replies (webmail: Settings > Mail > Automatic replies).
4. Find a file someone shared with me: office.com > Shared with me, or Teams > Files.
5. Book a meeting room: New meeting > Room Finder > pick the office.
6. Recover a deleted file: OneDrive or SharePoint > Recycle bin, kept for 93 days.

Escalate if: the requester needs a licence or feature that is off for their account (L2, software).
