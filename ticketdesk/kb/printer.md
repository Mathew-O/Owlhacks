# Printing and scanning on office printers

Category: printer
Owner: Service Desk (L1). Time: 10 minutes.

Symptoms: job shows sent but nothing prints, printer offline, scan to email never arrives.

1. Ask which printer (floor and office). New York 4th floor: NY-4-CANON. Philadelphia lobby: PHL-LOBBY-CANON.
2. Nothing prints: on the laptop, remove the printer and add it again from the print server (\\print.northline.com), then print a test page.
3. Job stuck for everyone: cancel the stuck job on the printer panel, or power cycle the printer (off, 30 seconds, on).
4. Printer offline on one laptop only: make sure the laptop is on the office network or VPN. Printers are not reachable from home Wi-Fi without VPN.
5. Scan to email not arriving: check the requester typed their own address, check the junk folder, and check the printer's date and time on the panel (wrong time breaks email sending).
6. Toner or paper: the office manager keeps stock, no ticket needed.

Escalate if: the printer is offline for everyone after a power cycle (hardware, L2), or scan to email fails for every user on that printer (L2).
