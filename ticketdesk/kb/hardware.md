# Laptops, monitors, docks and phones

Category: hardware
Owner: L2 desktop support, the Service Desk does the first checks. Time: same day in office, 2 days remote.

1. Will not turn on: hold the power button for 15 seconds, unplug the dock, plug the charger directly into the laptop, try again. No light at all after that means a swap from stock.
2. Only one monitor on the dock: unplug the dock from the laptop and from power for one minute, plug power first, then the laptop. Update the dock firmware from Software Center ("Dock Firmware"). Try the second monitor on the other dock port.
3. Slow laptop: restart (a real restart, not close the lid), check free disk space (needs 20 GB), check Windows updates pending.
4. Swap from stock: the Service Desk books a loaner, L2 moves the user's data with the standard migration, the broken device goes to repair.
5. Phone: SIM and carrier problems go to the office manager; app problems are handled like a laptop.

Escalate if: any hardware needs opening or replacing, or more than one user hit the same fault after an update (L2, possibly a rollback).
